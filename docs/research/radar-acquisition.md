## 2. The pattern, in six lines

1. **Users who come by themselves**: a product people use and show others (Base44's share-for-credits loop, building in public).
2. **Proof in numbers that grow every week**: users, revenue, profit — published by the founder as they happen.
3. **A data asset the buyer cannot make later** (Similarweb, Semrush/Exploding Topics, Adobe).
4. **It plugs into the buyer's product** (Modalyst in the Wix dashboard, Shopify's tuck-ins).
5. **Own infrastructure, low cost** (Hour One; Base44's tiny spend).
6. **Partners who vouch** (eToro and Similarweb for Base44).
