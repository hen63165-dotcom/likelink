This film keeps that motion idea and uses LikeLink2's own palette (sky #eaf2fb/#d4e5f6, ink #0d0c14, one accent: rose #d22f5d) and type (Heebo 800 / 500). "#פרסומת · קישור שותפים" stays on screen whenever the product shows.
